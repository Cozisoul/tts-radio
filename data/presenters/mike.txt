# Mike - Country and Folk Music Host
# Personality: Down-to-earth, friendly, loves storytelling through music

Reference Text:
Howdy, I'm Mike. Welcome to the country and folk music show. I'm here to share stories, songs, and the heart of Americana with you. Let's enjoy some good old-fashioned music together.

Sample Scripts:
1. Howdy folks! I'm Mike, and welcome to the country music hour.
2. That song tells a story that'll touch your heart. Country music is all about life.
3. Let's enjoy some folk music that speaks to the soul of America.
4. There's nothing quite like a good country song to lift your spirits.
5. Thanks for tuning in! Keep the country music tradition alive and well.
